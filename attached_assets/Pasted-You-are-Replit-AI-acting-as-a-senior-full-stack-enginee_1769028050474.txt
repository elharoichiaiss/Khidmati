================================================
APP OVERVIEW
================================================
App Name: Khidmati

Khidmati is a service marketplace that connects:
1) Clients (people looking for services)
2) Service Providers (electricians, plumbers, technicians, etc.)

This is an MVP:
- No payments
- No suppliers
- No GPS tracking
- Focus only on profiles, search, messaging, reviews

USER ROLES
================================================

1) CLIENT
- Can register and login
- Select city and service category
- Search for nearby service providers
- View provider profiles
- Send messages
- Leave reviews & ratings after service

2) SERVICE PROVIDER
- Can register and login
- Create and edit professional profile
- Add:
  - Service category
  - City / cities served
  - Bio
  - Years of experience
- Upload profile image
- Upload photos of completed works (portfolio)
- Receive messages
- View ratings and reviews

----------------------------

- updatedAt

----------------------------
Fields:
- userId (reference to User)
- serviceCategory
- bio
- yearsOfExperience
- citiesServed (array)
- profileImage (URL)
- portfolioImages (array of URLs)
- averageRating
- totalReviews
- isAvailable
- createdAt
- updatedAt

----------------------------
PORTFOLIO COLLECTION
----------------------------
Fields:
- providerId
- images (array of URLs)
- description
- createdAt

----------------------------
MESSAGING SYSTEM
----------------------------
Conversation collection:
- participants (userId array)
- lastMessage
- updatedAt

Message collection:
- conversationId
- senderId
- text
- createdAt
- readStatus

----------------------------
REVIEWS & RATINGS
----------------------------
Review collection:
- providerId
- clientId
- rating (1 to 5)
- comment
- createdAt

Rules:
- Only clients can submit reviews
- One review per completed service
- Reviews cannot be edited after submission
- Average rating updates automatically
- Admin can remove abusive reviews

  - City
  - Service category
- Sorted by:

  - Most active providers
